Privacy Policy

Last updated:

trakspend is a simple monthly budget tracker. We take privacy seriously and aim to collect as little information as possible.

Data We Collect

  • Personal Data: trakspend does not require you to create an account and does not collect personally identifiable information.
  • Budget & Expense Data: Your entries are stored on your device and may sync via Apple iCloud/CloudKit if you enable iCloud. This data is under your Apple ID and is not accessible to us.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

Analytics & Tracking

trakspend does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Security

Data is stored locally on your device and, if enabled, in your iCloud account, which is protected by Apple’s security controls.

Children

trakspend is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Contact

If you have questions or requests, email support@trakspend.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
